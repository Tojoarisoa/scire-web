      Welcome to SCIRE

      Source-grounded Coaching Intelligence through Reverse Engineering

      A coach that teaches you from your own material, and checks that you remember it.

      • Ask a question: the answer comes from your material, with a question to make you think.
      • Practice with questions made from it, graded as you answer.
      • Review topics just before you would forget them.
      • Progress shows what you have mastered.

      To start: create your account, then add material (a GitHub repository, files or notes). SCIRE shows each material's license: respect it.
